U.S. Community Safety Education

Understanding Gun Safety, Public Policy, and Community Protection

Why Gun Safety Matters

Gun violence affects schools, public places, and communities across the U.S.

Project Explanation

I made this project to help people quickly understand simple gun safety data because many students see news about shootings but don’t really know what the real numbers look like.

My project is mainly for high school and college students because they see this topic online a lot and need something easy to understand.

I use simple charts and short explanations so students can understand the information fast without feeling confused.

Gun violence affects schools and communities, but many people don’t realize that most Americans actually support basic safety rules.

Research from Johns Hopkins and Everytown shows that most Americans support safe storage, background checks, and that stronger gun laws usually mean fewer gun deaths.

My project shows that support for gun safety is higher than people think and that stronger laws can help make communities safer.
